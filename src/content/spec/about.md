# About

I am a postdoctoral researcher (Wissenschaftliche Mitarbeiterin) at the Chair of Practical Philosophy (Prof. Dr. Jörg Löschke), Institute of Philosophy, University of Stuttgart.

I received my PhD in Philosophy from the University of Zurich in 2024 (summa cum laude) with the dissertation *Widerhall und Spiegelung: Der vierstellige Begriff der Anerkennung*, supervised by Prof. Dr. Christoph Halbig, Prof. Dr. Jörg Löschke, and Prof. Dr. Axel Honneth. In fall 2019 I was a Visiting Scholar at the Department of Philosophy, Columbia University, at the invitation of Axel Honneth.

## Research Interests

- Recognition Theory
- Theories of Speech Acts
- Social Ontology
- Meta-Ethics

My current work brings recognition theory into conversation with speech act theory and social epistemology — asking what role the addressee's uptake plays in acts of recognition, and what a recognition-first perspective can tell us about credibility and epistemic injustice.
